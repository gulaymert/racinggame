# Game: Racing Game

### Game Features & Mechanics

**Player Car Controls**
- **Realistic Driving:** Smooth acceleration, braking, and steering.
- **Handbrake System:** Spacebar to drift and automatic stopping when no keys are pressed.
- **Anti-Flip Protection:** The car's weight is balanced at the bottom so it doesn't roll over easily.
- **Rescue Button:** Pressing 'R' instantly puts your car back on the road if you get stuck or fall off.

**Smart Enemy Car**
- **Auto-Driving:** The enemy automatically finds the best way to the finish line.
- **Aggressive Attacks:** The enemy actively tries to crash into you to slow you down.
- **Attack Cooldown:** The enemy waits a few seconds between attacks to keep the race fair.
- **Adjustable Difficulty:** The enemy's speed can be easily changed at any time.

**Realistic Crash Physics**
- **Slide & Spin:** Cars realistically slide and spin on the road instead of jumping.
- **Anti-Climb System:** Cars bump into each other like real metal, instead of climbing on top of each other.
- **Attacker Advantage:** If you hit the enemy, you keep your speed while they spin. If they hit you, you slow down.

**Finish Line & Screens**
- **Winner Detection:** The game perfectly detects who crossed the finish line first.
- **Time Freeze:** The race stops completely the moment someone wins.
- **Quick Restart:** A simple button to instantly restart the race without loading screens.
